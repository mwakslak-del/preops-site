Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website and our clinic

If any part of this site is hard for you to use, or you need information in another format, call or text 818-724-8200 and we will help you directly.

Last reviewed November 10, 2026.

What we aim for

We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. This site is built to be used with a keyboard, a screen reader, or enlarged text: pages use real headings and labels, links and buttons say what they do, text can be resized without breaking the layout, and colors are chosen for readable contrast.

Known limits

The downloadable referral form and scheduler quick reference are PDFs designed for printing and faxing, and they may not read well with a screen reader. The online referral form is provided by JotForm and may behave differently from the rest of the site. If either gets in your way, we will take the referral by phone or send the information another way.

Other ways to reach us

Everything on this website can also be done by phone. Call or text 818-724-8200, fax 818-724-8300, or email referrals@preops.com, Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM.

Visiting the clinic

If you need an accommodation for your visit, such as an interpreter, help with mobility, or extra time, tell us when we call to schedule or call us before your appointment so we can prepare.

Tell us about a problem

If you run into a barrier on this site, please tell us which page you were on and what happened. Call or text 818-724-8200 or email referrals@preops.com. We review every report and fix what we can.
