For Patients

Your Surgeon Asked for a Cardiac Evaluation. Here's What to Expect.

This is a focused visit to make sure your heart is ready for surgery.

Before Your Visit

  • Bring your medication list
  • Bring your insurance card and photo ID
  • Bring any prior heart records, if you have them

At Your Visit

  • We review your history and examine you
  • An ECG is done in the office
  • If more testing is needed, such as an echocardiogram (an ultrasound of the heart) or a treadmill or imaging stress test, we arrange it

After

  • We send the evaluation letter to your surgeon and your primary doctor
  • You'll be told exactly which medications to take or hold before surgery
  • Nothing for you to carry or deliver

How It Works

One Referral. We Handle the Rest.

01

Send the Referral

Fax the referral form with the patient's face sheet to 818-724-8300, or download the form at preops.com/refer. You can also email referrals@preops.com, or call and text 818-724-8200.

02

We Schedule and Evaluate

We call the patient directly, take the history, examine the patient, and arrange any needed cardiac testing: an echocardiogram, or a treadmill or imaging stress test.

03

Your Office Gets the Letter, the Consult, and the Reports

The evaluation letter, the consult note, and the reports of any testing go to the surgeon's office by fax, with a copy to the PCP. If anything could affect the surgery date, we call the office first.

For Surgeons' Offices

Built for Your Scheduler.

An evaluation that comes back late costs you an OR slot. The Preops Clinic exists so that doesn't happen: a single referral path, a human who answers the phone, and a letter that says what your anesthesiologist needs to know.

  • We Do the Scheduling

    Send the referral and we call the patient. Your staff doesn't chase anyone.

  • No Unnecessary Testing

    We follow ACC/AHA perioperative guidance, so a workup isn't ordered unless it changes the plan.

  • A Letter Your Anesthesiologist Can Use

    Risk assessment, medication instructions around surgery, and anticoagulant management, stated plainly, with the consult note and any test reports attached.

  • Patients Are Referred Back

    This is a preoperative service. Ongoing cardiac care, if needed, is coordinated with the patient's PCP and/or existing cardiologist.

About

A Service of Valley Heart and Rhythm

The Preops Clinic is the preoperative service of Valley Heart and Rhythm, a cardiology practice in Tarzana serving the San Fernando Valley. It was started by Menachem Wakslak, MD, a board-certified cardiologist, to give surgical patients a focused, timely evaluation. Any cardiac testing a patient needs before surgery is arranged by us.
