Privacy

How this website handles information

This page covers preops.com. The medical information we receive about patients is protected by HIPAA and handled under the Valley Heart and Rhythm Notice of Privacy Practices, which you can ask us for at any time.

Effective November 10, 2026.

What this website collects

You can read this website without giving us any information. It has no accounts, sets no cookies of its own, and runs no advertising or analytics trackers.

Like most websites, the companies that deliver our pages receive standard technical information when a page loads, such as your IP address and browser type. Our pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and our typeface is delivered by Google Fonts. We do not receive or use that information to identify you.

The online forms

The referral form on this site is provided through JotForm's HIPAA-compliant service. What a referring office enters there, including the patient's name, contact details, planned procedure, and medical information, is sent securely to our referral staff. We use it to contact and schedule the patient, to carry out the preoperative evaluation, and to send the results back to the referring office and the patient's primary care physician.

Patients can also use the appointment request form, which runs on the same HIPAA-compliant JotForm service. It asks only for what we need to call and schedule you: your name, date of birth, phone, surgeon, and insurance. We use it only to contact you about scheduling.

Email, text, and phone

If you email, call, or text us, we use your contact details to answer you. If you text us to request referral pads or forms, we use your number to respond to that request.

Ordinary email and text messages are not secure ways to send medical details. Referring offices should send patient information by fax to 818-724-8300 or through the online referral form. Patients should not put medical details in a text or email; call us instead.

How we use and share information

We use the information we receive to provide care and to run the clinic. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising. Patient information is shared only as HIPAA allows: with the referring surgeon's office, the patient's primary care physician and/or existing cardiologist, the patient's health plan, and the service providers who help us operate under agreements that require them to protect it.

Your health information rights

Patients have rights under HIPAA, including the right to see and get a copy of their records, to ask for corrections, and to know how their information has been shared. The Notice of Privacy Practices explains these rights. To ask for a copy of the notice or of your records, call 818-724-8200 or email records@preops.com.

Children

The Preops Clinic sees adults. This website is not directed to children.

Changes to this page

If we change how this website handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
